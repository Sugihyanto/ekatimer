Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

Meditation Timer ("the Application") is a free and open-source meditation timer developed by TipitakaPali.org ("the Service Provider").

The Application is designed to operate entirely on your device and does not require an account, internet connection, or registration.

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data.

Specifically, Meditation Timer is designed to:

  • Does not create user accounts.
  • Does not require sign-in.
  • Does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or identifiers.
  • Does not collect location data.
  • Does not collect device information.
  • Does not collect usage analytics.
  • Does not collect crash reports.
  • Does not use cookies.
  • Does not use advertising identifiers.
  • Does not display advertisements.
  • Does not sell or share user data with third parties.

All timer settings and preferences remain stored locally on your device.

Internet Connectivity

Meditation Timer is designed to function offline.

The Application does not transmit data to the Service Provider's servers and does not rely on cloud services for its core functionality.

Third-Party Services

The Application does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services.

Open Source

Meditation Timer is open-source software. The source code is publicly available at:

https://github.com/vpnry/ekatimer

Users are welcome to review the source code to verify how the Application operates.

Data Retention

Because the Application does not collect or transmit personal data, the Service Provider does not retain any personal information.

Any settings or preferences stored by the Application remain solely on the user's device and may be removed by uninstalling the Application.

Children's Privacy

The Application is suitable for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information from children or any other users.

Because no personal information is collected, there is no collection of children's personal information.

Parents and guardians who have questions regarding this Privacy Policy may contact the Service Provider using the contact information below.

Security

Since the Application does not collect or transmit personal information, the privacy risks associated with online data collection are minimized.

Any information stored by the Application remains on the user's device and is protected by the security mechanisms provided by the operating system.
