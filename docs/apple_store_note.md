3. App Purpose & Target Audience
----------------------------------
Meditation Timer is a lightweight, clean, and highly customizable meditation companion.
It provides meditation practitioners with highly accurate, on-device session timing and statistical metrics without digital distractions.

Key offerings include: 

• Three unique timer frameworks (Timed, target-focused End At, and open-ended Unlimited). 

• Fully customizable session audio configurations alongside vibration alerts. 

• Automated high-opacity visual dimming to turn the screen dark during meditation, saving battery life on OLED/LCD displays and keeping environments peaceful. 

• Streak tracking and visual statistics broken down by day, week, month, and year to support consistent habit formation.

• Quick-start home screen and lock screen widgets (static shortcuts) utilizing custom URL deep
links to instantly begin sessions.

Target Audience: Meditators, mindfulness practitioners, monastics, and anyone looking for a secure, offline tool to facilitate and analyze their daily sitting
practice.

5. External Services & Tools
-------------------------------
Meditation Timer is designed with a privacy-first, on-device architecture. It does not communicate with third-party servers, remote APIs, user analytics trackers, or artificial intelligence engines.

The app manages all computations locally: 
• Database storage: on-device SQLite database (sqflite). 
• Daily notifications: scheduled locally using standard system alarms. 
• Audio alerts: Notification sound paths (WAV files) are packed directly within the compiled app bundle and played through native system components. 
• Data Portability (CSV): Users can export their statistics or import legacy sessions through local .csv files. The app interacts solely with native iOS framework tools, user-generated data never leaves the device without explicit intent.
