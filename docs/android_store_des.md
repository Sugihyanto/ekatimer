**Meditation Timer — Designed for meditators**

Meditation Timer is a beautifully designed, full-featured meditation timer built for both beginners and experienced meditators. With support for multiple timer modes, rich sound options, haptic feedback, and detailed session tracking, Meditation Timer helps you build and maintain a consistent meditation practice.

**🕰️ Three Timer Modes**

- **Timed:** Set a precise duration from 1 minute to 10 hours. Choose from quick presets (1h, 1.5h, 2h, 3h), recently used durations, or use the slider for fine-grained control.
- **End At:** Set the timer to end at a specific time of day. Perfect for morning or evening practice sessions.
- **Unlimited:** Meditate freely without any time limit. The timer runs indefinitely until you choose to stop.

**🔔 Rich Audio & Haptic Feedback**

- 6 hand-picked meditation sounds: Bell, Bowl, Strong Bowl, Garden Bird, Gong, and Watch
- Configurable volume control (0–100%)
- Interval bells and mindfulness chimes at custom intervals (1–60 minutes)
- 5 vibration patterns: None, Short, Medium, Long, Double
- Sounds and vibrations play at session start, end, and during interval markers

**📊 Comprehensive Session Tracking**

- Automatic session history saved securely to your device
- Current streak and longest streak tracking to keep you motivated
- Detailed statistics: total sessions, total meditation time, average session duration
- Daily, weekly, and monthly meditation summaries
- Beautiful bar charts for weekly and monthly trends

**🎨 Beautiful Design**

- Zen-inspired color palette with calming teal and warm accent tones
- Full light and dark theme support (auto, light, dark)
- Smooth animations, fade transitions, and pulse effects
- Gradient backgrounds that create a serene meditation atmosphere
- Animated circular timer display with progress arc

**🔧 Customizable to Your Practice**

- Delay before session starts (0–60 seconds), with tap-to-skip
- Screen behavior: Stay On, Dim, or Normal during meditation
- Default timer mode and duration preferences
- Edit your fixed-hour preset buttons (customize the 4 quick-start options)
- Language support: English, Vietnamese, Burmese, Sinhala, German, Indonesian, Chinese, Thai, Hindi, Nepali, Korean, Japanese, Khmer, Russian, Lao

**📱 Home Screen Widgets**

Quick-start your meditation right from your home screen with dedicated Android widgets for 15m, 30m, 1h, 1.5h, 2h, 2.5h, 3h, 3.5h, 4h, End At, and Unlimited modes.

**⏰ Daily Reminders**

Set a daily reminder notification to never miss your practice. Configurable to any time that suits your schedule.

**🔒 Privacy First**

Your meditation data stays on your device. No accounts, no sign-ups, no data collection. Meditation Timer works completely offline.

**📜 Open Source**

Meditation Timer is free and open-source software licensed under GPLv3. Inspired by Meditation Assistant by Trevor Slocum.
